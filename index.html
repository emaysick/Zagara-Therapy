A person to work alongside you

Hi, I'm Emily.

I'm a Colorado-licensed clinical social worker specializing in OCD and anxiety, with completed training in OCD and Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) through the International OCD Foundation's Behavior Therapy Training Institute (BTTI), as well as EMDR training through the Trauma Training Institute.

I believe therapy can offer both compassion and challenge: a place to feel understood while building the flexibility to respond differently to what life brings. We'll work collaboratively, draw on evidence-based approaches, and keep your goals and values at the center of our work.
