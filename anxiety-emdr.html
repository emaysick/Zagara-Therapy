Online therapy for adults navigating persistent worry, avoidance, overwhelm, and the ways anxiety can pull you away from the life you want to live.

When anxiety starts making the decisions

Anxiety can show up as persistent worry, avoidance, feeling on alert, difficulty tolerating uncertainty, or getting caught in patterns that offer short-term relief but keep you feeling restricted. Therapy can help you understand those patterns and practice responding in ways that support your values and goals.

A collaborative, individualized approach

Our work begins with understanding what you're experiencing, what you've already tried, and what you want to be able to do differently. Together, we'll identify patterns that may be keeping you stuck and explore ways to respond with greater flexibility. Treatment may draw on evidence-based anxiety interventions and acceptance-based approaches, shaped around your needs, goals, and values.

Where EMDR may fit

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a structured psychotherapy approach that can help people process distressing experiences and the ways those experiences continue to affect them. It may be appropriate for certain trauma-related concerns, depending on your history, needs, and treatment goals.

We'll discuss whether EMDR makes sense for your circumstances and consider readiness and clinical fit together before deciding whether to incorporate it into treatment.

When OCD is the primary concern, OCD-specific treatment such as Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) remains central. EMDR does not replace evidence-based OCD treatment.

Start with a conversation

You don't have to know which approach you need before reaching out. We can talk about what brings you to therapy and consider whether working together may be a fit.
