Privacy

Your privacy matters.

A plain-language overview of this website’s information practices.

About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be handled when you visit the Zagara Therapy website or contact the practice through the options listed here. This is a website privacy notice; it is not the practice’s Notice of Privacy Practices for protected health information.

Information you choose to provide

This website does not currently include a general contact form or collect payment information. If you choose to email Emily using the email link on this website, the practice will receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your message. Email may not be a secure way to share sensitive information. Please avoid sending detailed personal or mental health information by ordinary email.

Sending an email or visiting this website does not, by itself, establish a therapist-client relationship.

Technical information and website hosting

This website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Like many website hosting services, the host and related infrastructure may process technical information associated with requests to the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, and timestamps, for delivery, reliability, and security purposes. The practice does not control every technical log generated by its hosting provider. For more information, review Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Fonts and external links

The site uses Google Fonts, which may cause your browser to request font resources from Google. Review Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s handling of data. This website may also link to third-party websites or services. Those services have their own privacy practices, which are not controlled by Zagara Therapy.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current website has no practice-operated contact form, payment checkout, or intentional advertising or analytics feature. Hosting or third-party services may still use technical processes or cookies as described in their own policies. If new tools are added in the future, this policy should be reviewed and updated to describe them accurately.

Therapy records and health information

Please do not use this website or ordinary email to send sensitive clinical information. If you become a client, information related to your care is handled under the applicable professional, privacy, and recordkeeping requirements and the practice’s separate client privacy documents. If HIPAA applies to the practice, the Notice of Privacy Practices explains how protected health information may be used and disclosed and describes your rights. This website policy does not replace that notice.

Children

This website is intended for adults seeking information about therapy services. It is not designed to solicit information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or practice procedures change. The date at the end of the policy indicates when it was last revised.

Questions

For questions about this website policy, email emily@zagaratherapy.com. Please avoid including sensitive personal or mental health details in your message.
